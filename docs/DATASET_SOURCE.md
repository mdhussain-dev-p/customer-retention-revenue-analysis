# Dataset source and attribution

Dataset: Online Retail II
Creator: Daqing Chen
Publisher: UCI Machine Learning Repository
DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D
Dataset page: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)

The UCI record describes 1,067,371 transactions from a UK-based registered
non-store online retailer, covering 1 December 2009 through 9 December 2011.
The business mainly sells gift-ware and includes customers who are wholesalers.

The original workbook is downloaded by `scripts/download_data.py` and is not
committed to Git. The workbook and generated SQLite database are excluded by
`.gitignore`; this keeps large raw and reproducible local files out of the
source repository.

Suggested citation:

Chen, D. (2012). Online Retail II [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository.
https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CG6D
