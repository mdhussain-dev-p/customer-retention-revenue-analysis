# Limitations

- The source covers one UK-based retailer and approximately two years. The
  results should not be generalised to all retailers.
- Customer-level analysis excludes transactions with no Customer ID. Those
  rows remain in revenue-only summaries.
- December 2011 is incomplete because the source ends on 9 December. It is
  excluded from monthly trend and cohort-retention comparisons.
- Cancellation records are treated as negative adjustments. Not every
  cancellation can be linked confidently to an original invoice.
- RFM is a descriptive summary of past behaviour, not a prediction of future
  customer value.
- Revenue is not profit. Product cost, taxes, marketing cost and fulfilment
  costs are not available in the workbook.
- The 5% reactivation calculation is an illustrative scenario, not a causal
  estimate or guaranteed outcome.
